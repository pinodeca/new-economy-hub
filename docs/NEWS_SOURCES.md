# News sources and aggregators

Where to look for news, events, and new organizations in this site's space
(new economy, cooperatives, solidarity economy, worker/employee ownership).
Use it as the starting point for research passes (`content/RESEARCH.md`)
and for the News-tab idea in `IDEAS.md`.

**Last checked: 2026-10-04**, via web search only — the agent sandbox's
network policy blocked direct fetches of these sites, so "active" below
means search results showed dated 2026 content, not that a page was loaded.
Re-check anything marked *unverified* before relying on it.

## Existing aggregators — how they compare

Nothing found is a comprehensive, structured, up-to-date aggregator of
this whole space. The closest things are curated newsletters, each with a
clear slant:

| Source | Focus | Coverage | Freshness (as of check) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [New Economy Roundup](https://neweconomycoalition.substack.com/) (New Economy Coalition, Substack) | Solidarity economy broadly, through a movement/justice lens; housing, land, mutual aid, co-ops, plus politics | Curated from 150+ member orgs; strong on US grassroots, weak on co-op business/sector news and employee ownership | Monthly; editions Feb, Apr, Sep 2026 seen |
| [Co-operative News](https://www.thenews.coop/) + ICA [Global News Hub](https://ica.coop/en/co-operative-news-global-news-hub-co-operatives) | Global co-op sector, all co-op types (retail, finance, agri, worker, housing) | The most comprehensive co-op *sector* news; UK-centric but global, 100+ contributors | Daily; some content is for members (£3/mo digital) |
| [The Stakehold](https://www.thestakehold.com/) (Substack) | Employee ownership in all forms — ESOPs, worker co-ops, EOTs, purpose trusts | Best weekly digest for the ownership side; its post ["All the Employee Ownership Newsletters"](https://www.thestakehold.com/p/all-the-employee-ownership-newsletters) is itself a source list | Weekly; paid spinoff "Ownership at Work" |
| [GEO — Grassroots Economic Organizing](https://geo.coop/) | Worker co-ops + solidarity economy; theory, movement news, "gleanings" link roundups | Broad, international, movement-oriented; long archive | Active; relaunched print GEO Journal Jan 2026; [weekly newsletter](https://newsletter.geo.coop/) |
| [Fifty by Fifty / Employee Ownership News](https://www.fiftybyfifty.org/employee-ownership-news/) (Democracy Collaborative) | Employee ownership news & research | Was a key aggregator; **stopped publishing on Medium** — current cadence *unverified* | Uncertain |
| [EOX "Ownership Everywhere"](https://www.eoxnetwork.org/newsletter) | US state employee-ownership centers (20+) | Narrow but good for state-level programs and events | Monthly; Sep 2026 edition seen |
| [USFWC "Worker Co-ops in the News"](https://www.usworker.coop/news-links/) | Press coverage of worker co-ops | Link list only, US + some international | *Unverified* cadence |
| [Socioeco.org](https://socioeco.org/index_en.html) (RIPESS) | Social & solidarity economy (SSE) library | 35k+ documents, 5 languages, aggregates 18 other sites — a library more than a news feed | Active |
| [Social Economy News](https://www.socialeconomynews.eu/en/) | EU social economy policy and institutions | Europe-only, policy-heavy | Active; issue #52 Jun 2026 |
| [Global Tapestry of Alternatives — *Weaving Alternatives*](https://globaltapestryofalternatives.org/) | Global post-development / alternatives, incl. solidarity economy | Very broad, Global South-weighted | Bimonthly |
| [Shelterforce — Solidarity Corner](https://shelterforce.org/content-type/solidarity-corner/) | Solidarity economy from housing/community development angle | Monthly column; Steve Dubb (formerly NPQ) | Active through Sep 2026 |

**Gap this site could fill:** a structured, filterable (geo, sector,
ownership model) view combining the movement lens of the NEC Roundup with
the sector lens of Co-op News and the ownership lens of The Stakehold —
and, specifically, *events and forums you can join*, which none of the
above organize systematically.

## Publications

- **[Nonprofit Quarterly](https://nonprofitquarterly.org/)** — strong co-op
  archive, but its main co-op writer Steve Dubb has moved to
  **[Shelterforce](https://shelterforce.org/author/steve-dubb/)**; follow him there.
- **[Shareable](https://www.shareable.net/)** — commons, mutual aid, co-ops; active.
- **[Next City](https://nextcity.org/)** — urban/community-wealth angle; regular worker co-op and CLT stories.
- **[Resilience.org — Economy](https://www.resilience.org/economy/)** — republishes co-op/new-economy pieces (e.g. Brazil co-ops, May 2026).
- **[Dollars & Sense](https://www.dollarsandsense.org/)** — bimonthly left economics; active (Sep/Oct 2026 issue).
- **[YES! Magazine](https://www.yesmagazine.org/topic/economy/cooperatives)** — ⚠️ **ceased regular publication in 2025**; now a monthly newsletter of archive + curated pieces. Archive is still useful; not a news source.
- **[The Next System Project](https://thenextsystem.org/)** — mostly an archive (reader, teach-ins, podcast); not a news source.
- International: [Co-op News Kenya](https://co-opnews.com/) (African co-ops/SACCOs, unrelated to the UK title), [The Cooperator News](https://thecooperator.news/), [Indian Cooperative](https://indiancooperative.com/).

(institute.coop, USFWC's sister org).

## Social media

**Bluesky is now the main platform for this movement.** Many co-op orgs
publicly left X (USFWC explained why in
[a blog post](https://www.usworker.coop/blog/join-the-worker-co-op-conversation-on-bluesky/)).

- **Bluesky:** [@cooperatives.bsky.social](https://bsky.app/profile/cooperatives.bsky.social) ("The Cooperative Economy", ~27k followers — the biggest general account), @usworker.coop, @geocollective.bsky.social. Starter packs exist for US worker co-ops and UK worker co-ops (60 accounts) — follow these to bootstrap.
- **Mastodon:** [social.coop](https://social.coop/) — a member-owned co-op instance, active in 2026; its local timeline is co-op-heavy. GEO is @GEO_Collective@social.coop; workers.coop is also on Mastodon.
- **X:** @coopnews, @USFWC still exist but are less active post-migration.
- **LinkedIn:** where the employee ownership / ESOP world (NCEO, EOX, advisors) and the ICA / European social economy bodies post most. Requires an account to read beyond a few posts.

## Forums / two-way communities

- [social.coop](https://social.coop/) (Mastodon + [Loomio](https://www.loomio.com/socialcoop)).
- **Reddit — *unverified*:** r/cooperatives is the commonly cited subreddit; r/workercoops / r/WorkerCooperatives / r/ESOP may exist. Reddit was unreachable from both the sandbox and the search tool, so a human needs to check names and activity.
