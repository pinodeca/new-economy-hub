# News sources and aggregators

Where to look for news, events, and new organizations in this site's space
(new economy, cooperatives, solidarity economy, worker/employee ownership).
Use it as the starting point for research passes (`content/RESEARCH.md`)
and for the News-tab idea in `IDEAS.md`.

**Last checked: 2026-10-13.** Checked via web search only. The agent
sandbox's network policy blocks direct fetches of these sites, so the date
next to each source is the newest dated item search turned up, not a
reading of the live page.

## Freshness rule

**A source stays on this list only if it has published something in the
last 3 months.** Anything older goes under [Dropped](#dropped-stale) with
its last-seen date, so it isn't rediscovered and re-added later. Re-run the
check before each research pass that leans on this list.

Search can prove a source is alive, but it can't prove a source is dead: a
missing result may just mean the page isn't indexed. So a source is only
dropped on positive evidence (an announced shutdown, or a feed whose
newest entry is old). Sources where search simply found nothing recent go
under [Couldn't verify](#couldnt-verify), for a human to check.

Directories and libraries (maps, document archives) aren't news, so the
rule doesn't apply to them. They're listed separately under
[Reference](#reference-not-news).

**Gap this site could fill:** a structured, filterable view (geography,
sector, ownership model) that combines the movement lens of the NEC
Roundup, the sector lens of Co-op News and the ownership lens of The
Stakehold. In particular, *events and forums you can join*, which none of
the above organize systematically.

## Social media

**Bluesky is now the main platform for this movement.** Many co-op orgs
publicly left X; USFWC explained why in
[a blog post](https://www.usworker.coop/blog/join-the-worker-co-op-conversation-on-bluesky/).

- **Bluesky:**
  - @usworker.coop: Oct 2, 2026.
  - @geocollective.bsky.social: Sep 2026.
  - Starter packs exist for US and UK worker co-ops; following one is the quickest way to pick up many accounts.
- **X:** @coopnews and @USFWC still exist, but are less active since the move to Bluesky.
- **LinkedIn:** where the employee-ownership / ESOP world (NCEO, EOX, advisors) and the ICA / European social economy bodies post most. Requires an account to read more than a few posts.

## Forums / two-way communities

- [forum.workers.coop](https://forum.workers.coop/): Discourse forum of the UK worker co-op federation; active (Autumn Assembly 2026 threads). Some categories are members-only.

## Podcasts / video

- [Laura Flanders & Friends](https://lauraflanders.org/): PBS; co-op episodes in 2026. In Feb 2026 production slowed to focus on a "best of" compilation; follow her Substack for current output.

## Reference (not news)
